Resources on Writing
--------------------

{{% resource_link "f4d9cb01-0869-4a1a-b99c-a053dd0d8ac7" "Purdue Online Writing Lab (OWL)" %}} – An excellent, succinct resource on questions of grammar and sentence structure (aka "mechanics").

{{% resource_link "1ed2611e-306e-4279-acd3-710863eb6eb4" "On Making Good Sentences" %}} – "Where Do Sentences Come From?" by NY Times writer Verlyn Klinkenborg.

"{{% resource_link "167162a7-7272-4fd3-887a-78d37b7a1b34" "No One Writes Alone" %}}" – Video on peer review in the classroom from MIT Tech TV.

{{% resource_link "778706ad-ea5e-4525-9a28-c5dda5dc1b33" "On Writing Scenes" %}} – A recent NY Times article by Lee Gutkind on why and how to use scenes in non-fiction writing.

{{% resource_link "b7744503-1ede-41c7-92e6-eb03be1c0b09" "What do we Mean by an Argument?" %}} – An excellent discussion of what we mean by terms such as argument, claim, evidence, and counterargument in essays such as our Essay 2 and 3—and most essays you will write in college. It is from the Writing Center at UNC-Chapel Hill.

{{% resource_link "674f7fb3-848f-423f-82f6-3de6846ff679" "Thesis" %}} – A good explanation of what a thesis is and how it works in an essay. This site also contains links to additional sites on "thesis".

{{% resource_link "5ce745db-e848-4b08-b1c2-7e651eff3a87" "Taylor Mali on speaking with conviction" %}} – Youtube clip

These two pieces about punctuation are especially useful because they're from the point of view of writers (not teachers or grammarians):

"{{% resource_link "fd35e02b-2dfd-4c05-ab28-d36b64235e5d" "The Most Comma Mistakes" %}}" by Ben Yagoda.

"{{% resource_link "e66ffede-6b84-456a-85af-bc6d1ef96cbb" "Semi-colons: A Love Story" %}}" by Ben Dolnic.

Resources for Research
----------------------

{{% resource_link "e01ae6a3-3d57-4573-8a0b-7790c2b85eed" "Purdue OWL Resources for Writing Researched Papers" %}} – A very useful—and clearly organized—compendium of all kinds of help, from how to get started to why and how to write definitions of words, to MLA and APA style guides.

"{{% resource_link "ad09eb78-aa6e-47ed-9d26-a4e1dd916122" "Research and Documentation Online" %}}" – An excellent source for research. It includes lists of databases by subject area, as well as brief guides to styles such as MLA, APA, etc.

{{% resource_link "7d0f0c9c-ddbc-408d-99e0-4d77dc60f506" "Fact Check.org" %}} – A project of the Annenberg Public Policy Center, mostly for what's in the news, especially politics.

{{% resource_link "a69b7894-0aee-42b9-829f-741039cc947e" "Snopes.com" %}} – Their mission is to check out "rumors" of all kinds, including folklore, urban legends and misinformation.

{{% resource_link "656b6174-da9e-4537-9418-0ccc724f663a" "Frontline" %}} – The PBS documentary series has been running for over 30 years, and covers a variety of topics, from science & technology to current events. The site has its own search engine of documentaries by topic.

{{% resource_link "75a5076e-3613-4fc9-823e-8a899bd09d66" "ProPublica" %}} – An independent, non-profit organization that investigates issues with "moral force." It won the 2011 Pulitzer Prize for National Reporting.
